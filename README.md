# GradeDash

## Project Overview
GradeDash is an AI-powered tool designed to help students become more resilient in their educational endevours. Our solution leverages Claude’s advanced AI capabilities to provide an interactive, adaptive, and gamified learning experience which can be catered to each student’s specific needs.

##  Problem Statement
Learning can feel really boring sometimes, and it’s easy for kids to get distracted. Traditional ways of teaching—like long lectures, endless memorization, or simply copying notes—often make students tune out. Before you know it, their minds are wandering, they’re losing focus, and they start associating school with frustration instead of curiosity. Over time, this can make learning feel like a chore rather than something exciting.

To truly engage students, we need methods that are interactive, creative, and fun—ways that make learning something they actually look forward to.

## Solution: AI-Powered Gamified Studying
We are reimagining studying by turning it into a fully gamified, interactive experience that adapts to each student’s unique way of learning.

At the heart of our platform is an AI-powered system that generates multiple-choice questions tailored to the student’s preferences. Students can decide not just the topic, but also the style of the questions—they can choose serious, challenging questions, or make learning fun with funny, memey, or pop-culture-inspired MCQs. This gives them a sense of control over their learning, making it personal and engaging.

Students can also upload their own PDFs or study materials, allowing the AI to automatically generate personalized MCQs. For those who prefer ready-made content, we provide an extensive database of questions across different topics.

The game structure ensures that students remain focused, preventing attention from drifting, while features like points, rewards, and a local leaderboard encourage healthy competition and long-term motivation.

Our goal is to make learning feel less like a chore and more like a challenge that’s fun, interactive, and rewarding. By blending AI customization with gamification, we create a space where students genuinely enjoy testing their knowledge, retain information more effectively, and build confidence in their abilities—all while having fun and staying engaged.


### Key Features

- **AI-Generated Questions**
  - Students choose the topic (e.g., “Linear Algebra Prelim 2,” “Biology Unit 3,” etc.).
  - AI generates 4-option MCQs from:
    - Uploaded PDFs
    - Pasted notes
    - Or pre-existing shared sets
  - Questions can be:
    - Exam-style and rigorous  
    - Light, humorous, or meme-based

- **Uploads & Custom Content**
  - Upload your own **PDFs** or paste text.
  - The AI parses the content and creates personalized MCQs based on what *you* actually need to study.

- **Built-In Question Bank**
  - For quick play, users can search and reuse **existing sets** across different topics.

- **Game Mechanics**
  - Lane-based runner: dodge distractions, move into the correct lane (A/B/C/D) to answer.
  - **Points** increase with correct answers and streaks.
  - **Streaks** and subtle “A+” feedback reinforce resilience and focus.
  - A **local leaderboard** encourages friendly competition and long-term motivation.


With this approach, we’re transforming how students experience learning itself.

While platforms like Quizlet offer pre-made flashcards and Duolingo gamifies language learning, both are limited in personalization and content flexibility. Our platform combines the best of both worlds: it generates tailored multiple-choice questions from uploaded PDFs or text, allowing students to learn in their preferred style—serious, funny, or even memey. Unlike Quizlet or Duolingo, which rely on pre-set content, our AI ensures the material is accurate, relevant, and engaging. The gamified format, with points and a local leaderboard, keeps students focused and motivated, turning studying into a fun, interactive experience rather than a passive task.

## Claude AI Integration:
We integrated AI at the core of our platform to make the learning experience truly personalized. The AI parses PDFs and any text uploaded by the user to automatically generate multiple-choice questions tailored to their needs and preferences. It also ensures the integrity of the content by fact-checking that the questions and answers are accurate and reliable.

Additionally, we leveraged Claude to assist us in coding, helping streamline development and implement features more efficiently. By combining AI-driven content generation, personalization, and verification, we ensure that every user gets a fun, engaging, and trustworthy learning experience.
